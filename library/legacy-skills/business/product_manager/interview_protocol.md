# 🎙️ Product Manager Skill: Deep Job Discovery Protocol

This skill guides the AI to conduct a "Deep Job Discovery" interview, modeled after the GoPractice "Handy" case study.

## 🎯 Goal
To uncover the "Job to be Done" by focusing on the **specific purchase moment** rather than general opinions.

## 🗣️ Protocol Script (The "Handy" Method)

### Phase 1: The Purchase Moment (Context)
*   **Trigger:** "Tell me about the specific day/moment you bought [Product]."
*   **Probing:** "Where were you? What time was it? Who was with you? What happened just before?"
*   **Goal:** Reconstruct the *circumstance*.

### Phase 2: The Struggle & Alternatives (Competition with Non-Consumption)
*   **Trigger:** "If you hadn't bought [Product], what would you have done instead?"
*   **Probing:** "Did you look at other solutions? Did you consider doing nothing? Why did you reject them?"
*   **Goal:** Identify the *real competitors* (including Excel, pen & paper, or apathy).

### Phase 3: The Expectations (Push)
*   **Trigger:** "What specifically did you expect to happen after buying?"
*   **Probing:** "What was the 'Job' you were hiring this product to do? Was it to save time? To look cool? To stop worrying?"
*   **Goal:** Define the *functional* and *emotional* job.

### Phase 4: The Value (Pull)
*   **Trigger:** "Why is [Product] better than the alternative you mentioned?"
*   **Probing:** "What is the *specific* added value?"
*   **Goal:** Calculate the *Delta* (Value of New Solution > Cost of Change + Value of Old Solution).

## 📝 Output Format
After the interview, summarize:
1.  **The Trigger:** (Event that started the search)
2.  **The Job:** (When [Context], I want to [Motivation], so I can [Outcome])
3.  **The Big Hire:** (Why they chose us)
4.  **The Little Fire:** (Why they fired the old solution)
