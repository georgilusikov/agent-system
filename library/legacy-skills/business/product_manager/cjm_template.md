# 🗺️ Product Manager Skill: CJM Generator

This skill converts Job Stories and User Research data into a structured Customer Journey Map (CJM).

## 🎯 Goal
To visualize the user's "As-Is" journey (current struggle) vs. the "To-Be" journey (with our product).

## 🧩 Template Structure

### The "As-Is" Journey (The Struggle)
*Map the user's current experience trying to solve the problem without our product.*

| Stage | User Goal | Touchpoint / Tool | Emotion | Pain Points | Opportunities |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|---------|-------------|---------------|
| **Trigger** | (What starts the need?) | (e.g., Email, Boss, Error) | 😐 | (Frustrations) | (How to intervene?) |
| **Search** | (Looking for solutions) | (Google, Friends, Excel) | 😟 | (Confusion, Noise) | (SEO, Content) |
| **Choice** | (Deciding what to do) | (Pricing page, comparison) | 😰 | (Complexity, Cost) | (Simplification) |
| **Consumption** | (Trying to solve it) | (Manual work, Old tool) | 😫 | (Slow, Errors) | (Automation) |
| **Result** | (Did it work?) | (Report, Outcome) | 🤷 | (Uncertainty) | (Guaranteed result) |

### The "To-Be" Journey (The Ideal Flow)
*Map the ideal experience with our proposed solution.*

| Stage | User Action | Product Feature | Emotion | Added Value |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|---------|-------------|
| **Onboarding** | (Sign up / Setup) | (Magic Link, Import) | 🤩 | (Speed) |
| **First Use** | (Solving the core job) | (One-click action) | 🤯 | (Zero effort) |
| **Habit** | (Regular usage) | (Notifications, Reports) | 😎 | (Control) |

## 🧠 Instruction for AI
1.  Read the **Job Stories** from the previous step.
2.  Identify the **biggest pain points** in the "As-Is" journey.
3.  Propose a "To-Be" journey that explicitly removes the **negative emotions**.
4.  Fill the tables above.
