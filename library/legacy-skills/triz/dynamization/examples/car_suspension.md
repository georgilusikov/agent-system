# Example: Dynamization - Car Suspension

## Input
"Classic car suspension is a compromise. Rigid suspension provides good handling but poor comfort (shaking). Soft suspension is comfortable but causes the car to lean in turns."

## Execution Trace

### 1. Dynamization Chain
1. **Rigid Link:** Solid axle (Stage 1).
2. **One Hinge:** Simple spring/shock absorber (Stage 2).
3. **Multi-link:** Sophisticated geometry (Stage 3).
4. **Fluid:** Hydraulic/Pneumatic systems (Liquid/Gas Stage).
5. **Field:** Magneto-Rheological (MR) Fluid + Magnetic Field (Final Stage).

### 2. Selection of Solution
- Use **MR Fluid** in the shock absorbers.
- The fluid contains iron particles. In a magnetic field, they align, making the fluid "solid" (rigid suspension). Without the field, it is a liquid (soft suspension).
- **Result:** The system adapts millisecond by millisecond based on road sensors.

## Output
- **Concept:** Intelligent Adaptive Suspension.
- **Trend Applied:** Transition from Rigid to Field-controlled state.
- **Result:** Maximum comfort AND maximum stability simultaneously.
