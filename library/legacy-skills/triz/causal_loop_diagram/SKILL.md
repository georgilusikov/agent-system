# Causal Loop Diagram

Use this skill when the issue is chronic, recurring, or systemic and simple linear cause-effect is not enough.

This skill is best for:
- repeated inefficiencies that come back after local fixes
- organizational or process problems with delays and side effects
- situations where one improvement worsens another part of the system later

Follow `core_instructions.md` exactly and finish with leverage points plus a clear handoff.
