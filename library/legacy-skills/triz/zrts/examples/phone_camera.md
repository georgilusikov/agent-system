# Example: ZRTS - Smartphone Camera Evolution

## Input
"Predict the next step for smartphone cameras. We currently have 3-4 lenses on the back."

## Execution Trace (Evolutionary Trends)

### 1. Current State (S-Curve)
- Stage 2 (Growth / Poly-system transition).
- We move from **Mono** (1 lens) to **Poly** (2, 3, 4 lenses: wide, tele, macro).

### 2. Physical Limits
- We cannot add 10 or 20 lenses (size, weight, cost). This is the "Saturation of Poly-systems".

### 3. Transition to Micro-level
- **ZRTS Law:** Transition from Macro-parts (glass lenses) to Micro-structure / Fields.
- **Trend:** **Computational Photography**.
- **Action:** Instead of hardware zoom (moving lenses), we use AI super-resolution and sensor cropping (Information replaces Glass).

### 4. Next Step: Integration
- Integration of "Inverse Systems". One camera captures depth, another captures color, AI merges them.

## Output
- **Prediction:** The number of physical lenses will stop growing. The next jump is to **Light Field Cameras** (capturing all directions) or **AI-integrated synthetic imaging**, where "The Lens" is a software construct.
- **Result:** Flat phones with SLR-quality output.
