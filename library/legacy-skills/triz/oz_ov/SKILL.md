# 🎯 OZ-OV (Operational Zone & Time)

**Purpose:** Pinpoint exact location and moment of conflict.

**When to use:**
- Before ARIZ (Step 1.4)
- Narrowing down problem scope
- Finding minimal intervention zone

**Output:** OZ coordinates, OV timeline, reformulated problem

**Gate Check:**
- OZ narrowed to millimeter/molecule level
- OV split into T1 (conflict), T2 (before), T3 (after)
- Problem reformulated for minimal intervention
