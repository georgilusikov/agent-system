# Example: Standards - Ice Crushing on Ship Hull

## Input
"Ice (S1) sticks to the ship hull (S2), causing friction and increasing fuel consumption. Mechanical scraping is too energy intensive."

## Execution Trace (Vepol Standards)

### 1. Diagnosis
- **S1:** Ice.
- **S2:** Hull.
- **F:** Mechanical field (Friction).
- **Type:** **Inconvenient Vepol** (S1-S2 interaction is inefficient).

### 2. Standard Search (Class 2: Evolution)
- **Standard 2.2.1:** Replacing current field F1 with field F2 (more efficient energy interaction).
- **Options:**
    - F1 (Mechanical) -> F2 (Thermal).
    - F1 (Mechanical) -> F2 (Acoustic/Vibration).

### 3. Implementation Concept
- **Solution A:** Use waste heat from engines to heat the hull (Thermal Field).
- **Solution B:** Install piezoelectric vibrators on the hull (Vibration Field).
- **Physics:** High frequency vibration creates a "gas/liquid layer" between ice and hull, breaking the bond.

## Output
- **Standard used:** 2.2.1 (Field Replacement).
- **Selected Solution:** Acoustic/Vibration de-icing.
- **Result:** Drastic reduction in hull friction without physical scraping.
