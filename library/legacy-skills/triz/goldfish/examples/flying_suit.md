# Example: Personal Anti-Gravity Suit

## Input
"I want a suit that lets people fly like Superman, just by thinking about it. No jetpacks, no wings, just pure flight."

## Execution Trace
1.  **Sandwich:**
    - Real: Suit, Thinking control (BCI exists), People exist.
    - Fantasy: "Pure flight" without propulsion/wings (Anti-gravity).

2.  **Recursive Interrogation:**
    - *Why is it fantasy?* Because gravity pulls 80kg mass down with 800N force, and we have nothing pushing up.
    - *Why impossible?* Because Newton's 3rd Law: Action requires Reaction. Anti-gravity doesn't exist.
    - *Root Barrier:* We need Force Up without Mass Ejection (Reaction).

3.  **Translation:**
    - How to generate 800N lift without carrying fuel/engines?
    - Contradiction: Suit must be **Heavy** (to carry engines) and **Light/Invisible** (to create "pure flight" feeling).

4.  **Analogs:**
    - *Nature:* Spiders (Ballooning - using electrostatic fields).
    - *Industry:* Maglev (Magnetic repulsion - but needs track).
    - *Simulated:* Zero-G plane (Parabolic flight - falling).

## Output
## 🧜‍♀️ Goldfish Analysis: Superman Suit

### 1. The Sandwich
- **Real Shell:** Neural interface (BCI) for control; A suit structure.
- **Fantasy Core:** Levitation without reaction mass (Violation of Newton's 3rd Law).

### 2. Recursive Interrogation
- **Why is it fantasy?**
  - Because gravity applies 800N force downwards, and there is no equal upward force.
- **Why is that impossible?**
  - Because usually Upward Force requires reaction mass (Fuel/Air), which is bulky/visible.
- **Root Barrier:** Generating Lift without onboard Reaction Mass.

### 3. Translation to Reality
**Physical Contradiction:**
"The Propulsion System must be **Present** (to provide lift) and **Absent** (to preserve 'pure flight' experience)."

**Hypothesis used:**
- Separation in Space: Move the propulsion *external* to the user?
- **Concept:** Skydiving Tunnel (External airflow supports user).
- **Concept:** Magnetic Levitation (External field supports user - requires suit with superconductors).

### 4. Analogs (FOS)
- **Nature:** Spider Ballooning (using Earth's electric field for lift).
- **Industry:** Drone Swarm (Concept: A swarm of micro-drones under the feet/back, invisible from distance).
