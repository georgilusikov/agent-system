# Example: Goldfish - The Flying House

## Input
"I want a house that can fly, so I can travel without leaving my home."

## Execution Trace (De-Magic Protocol)

### 1. Real Components
- A house provides shelter, weight (~100 tons), fixed location, volume.
- Environment has Gravity and Air.

### 2. The "Magic" Core
What exactly is magical? "A structure of 100 tons floats in the air without consuming massive fuel."

### 3. Localization of Miracle
The magic is in **Weight Compensation**.

### 4. Technical Analogies
- **Aerostatics:** Balloons (Archimedes principle).
- **Aerodynamics:** Lift from wings (Needs high speed).
- **Magnetic:** Maglev (Needs tracks/fields).

### 5. Concept Synthesis
- **Draft Contradiction:** "The House must be Heavy/Solid (for safety/shelter) and Light (for flight)."
- **Strategy:** Separation in Structure.
- **Hypothesis:** A "Flying Suitcase" house. The living area is a lightweight composite container, integrated with a helium envelope (Blimp-house).
- **Result:** Hybrid Airship-House.

## Output
- **Solid Core:** A modular lightweight house.
- **The Miracle:** Buoyancy.
- **Direction:** -> Take to @contradictions ("Large volume for lift vs Small volume for transport").
