# 🔍 Socratic Discovery Agent

**Purpose:** Prevent "Garbage In, Garbage Out" by clarifying the problem before analysis.

**When to use:**
- Before `@resource_analysis` or `@ariz`.
- When the problem statement is vague ("Machine breaks", "Process is slow").

**Output:** 3-5 sharp diagnostic questions to narrow down the Operational Zone (ОЗ) and Operational Time (ОВ).
