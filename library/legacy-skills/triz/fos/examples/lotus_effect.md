# Example: FOS - Self-Cleaning Lenses (Lotus Effect)

## Input
"Camera lenses on outdoor equipment get dirty from dust and water spots. Wiping them is not possible (too many cameras) or risky (scratches). How to keep them clean automatically?"

## Execution Trace (Function Oriented Search)

### 1. General Function
"How to remove solid and liquid debris from a transparent surface without mechanical wiping?"

### 2. Extreme Industry Search
- **Medical:** Need sterile surfaces that don't collect bacteria? (Too small).
- **Space:** Lenses must remain clean for years in vacuum? (Resource-heavy).
- **Nature (Biomimetics):** How do plants stay clean in the mud?

### 3. Bionic Discovery
- **The Lotus Leaf:** Water droplets stay as perfect beads and roll off, taking all dust with them.
- **Physical Principle:** "Micro-roughness" (tiny bumps on a wax surface) prevents the drop from sticking.

### 4. Adaptation (Transfer)
- Use **Nanostructuring** (Nanobumping) or special hydrophobic coatings.
- **Action:** Instead of "cleaning" the lens, we change its surface properties so dirt cannot stick in the first place.

## Output
- **Concept:** Self-cleaning hydrophobic lens coating.
- **Analogy:** Lotus Effect.
- **Result:** Dirt is washed away by rain; no manual maintenance needed.
