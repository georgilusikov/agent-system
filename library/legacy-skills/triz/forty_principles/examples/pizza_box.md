# Example: 40 Principles - Pizza Box (Condensation Problem)

## Input
"Hot pizza in a cardboard box creates steam. The steam makes the crust soggy (Harm). If we add holes to the box, the pizza gets cold too fast. We need it to stay Hot AND Dry."

## Execution Trace

### 1. Contradiction
The box must be **Sealed** (to keep heat) and **Vented** (to remove steam).

### 2. Matching Principles
- **Principle #1 (Segmentation):** Small holes? (No, still loses heat).
- **Principle #17 (Another Dimension):** Change the geometry of the contact area.

### 3. Solution Concept
- **Idea:** Raise the pizza above the floor of the box.
- **Action:** Create a **corrugated/ridged bottom** for the cardboard box.
- **Logic:** Steam flows into the "valleys" (Another Dimension - depth), while the pizza sits on the "peaks" (ridges).
- **Result:** The pizza crust stays dry because it's not sitting in its own puddle, and the box stays closed to trap the heat.

## Output
- **Principle used:** #17 (Another Dimension).
- **Result:** Hot and crispy pizza. Standard in modern high-end pizza delivery.
