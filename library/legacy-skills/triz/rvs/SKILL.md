# 📐 RVS (Размер - Время - Стоимость)

**Purpose:** Break mental blocks through extreme scaling experiments.

**When to use:**
- Need paradigm shift
- Stuck in conventional thinking
- Looking for radical alternatives

**Output:** 6 extreme experiments report, key insight, paradigm recommendation

**Gate Check:**
- All 6 extremes tested (not just 10x)
- Insight translated back to engineering
- infinity (∞) reached, not just "big"
